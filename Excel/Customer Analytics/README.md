# 📊 Customer Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Customer Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze customer acquisition, revenue performance, churn, retention, segmentation, and campaign response.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to understand customer behavior and identify key business opportunities by analyzing:

- Customer acquisition channels
- Revenue generated by each channel
- Customer churn and retention
- Customer segmentation
- Cohort trends
- Campaign response
- At-risk customer groups

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
- Excel Formulas
- Data Analysis & Visualization

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Customer Acquisition
Analyzed customer acquisition across different channels:

- YouTube – 187 customers
- Walk-in – 182 customers
- Instagram – 165 customers
- Google – 160 customers
- Facebook – 155 customers
- Referral – 151 customers

### 2. Customer Status

Customer base is divided into:

- **Churned:** 75%
- **At Risk:** 18%
- **Active:** 7%

### 3. Revenue by Acquisition Channel

Revenue performance across acquisition channels was analyzed to identify the highest and lowest contributing channels.

- **YouTube:** ₹1.22M – Highest revenue
- **Walk-in:** ₹1.10M
- **Instagram:** ₹1.00M
- **Google:** ₹0.99M
- **Facebook:** ₹0.90M
- **Referral:** ₹0.87M – Lowest revenue

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented into four membership categories:

- Silver – 27%
- Bronze – 25%
- Platinum – 25%
- Gold – 23%

### 5. Cohort Month Trend

Monthly customer trends were analyzed to identify changes in customer acquisition and engagement over time.

### 6. Campaign Response

Campaign response was compared across acquisition channels to identify channels generating the highest customer participation.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **75% of customers are churned**, indicating significant customer loss and a strong need for retention strategies.
- **YouTube generated the highest revenue (₹1.22M)** and also acquired the highest number of customers (187).
- **Referral generated the lowest revenue (₹0.87M)**, indicating an opportunity to improve referral campaigns.
- **18% of customers are At Risk**, requiring targeted engagement and retention campaigns.
- Customer distribution is relatively balanced across membership segments, with **Silver customers representing the largest segment at 27%**.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- **Reduce churn by 10%** to potentially retain approximately **100 additional customers**.
- Increase **YouTube campaign investment by 10–15%** to strengthen customer acquisition from the highest-performing channel.
- Improve referral incentives and campaigns to increase customer acquisition and revenue.
- Target the **180 At-Risk customers** with personalized offers, loyalty benefits, and re-engagement campaigns.
- Monitor cohort trends regularly to identify declining customer engagement early.
