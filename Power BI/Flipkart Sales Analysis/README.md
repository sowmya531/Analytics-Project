# 📈 Flipkart Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Flipkart sales performance by tracking revenue, product categories, customer ratings, regional sales, payment methods, and sales trends. It enables data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Analyze customer ratings and satisfaction.
- Understand payment method preferences.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Sales Performance Dashboard
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Average Customer Rating
- Quantity Sold
- Region-wise Sales Analysis
- Product Category Performance
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Payment Method Distribution
- Customer Rating Analysis
- Interactive Slicers

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- South Region generated the highest sales revenue.
- Grocery emerged as the highest-selling product category.
- Debit Card was the most preferred payment method.
- Beauty & Health products received the highest customer ratings.
- Sales remained stable throughout the analysis period.
